Questions

Good to know before you start

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp.

How much does a website cost?

Every project gets a fixed quote based on the pages and features you need. You know the full price before any work starts, with no hidden fees.

How long does it take?

Most business websites take two to four weeks from approved design to launch. Larger sites with booking or payments can take longer. We agree a date upfront.

Can you redesign my current website?

Yes. We can redesign an outdated site, fix a slow or broken one, or recover a site whose domain has expired.

Can I add 3D like the car and house above?

Yes. Interactive 3D works well for car dealers, real estate and product brands. We'll advise if it suits your site and keep it fast on phones.

Do I own the website?

Yes. Your domain, website files and content belong to you. There is no lock in.

How do I pay?

By Payoneer, Cleva, Wise, or bank transfer to accounts in Nigeria, the UK and the USA. Details are on your invoice.
