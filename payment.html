Policies

Payment policy

We keep payments simple and transparent. You get a fixed quote before any work starts, and you never pay for anything you didn't agree to.

Last updated 4 November 2026

Quotes

After you send your brief, we send a written fixed quote that lists exactly what's included: pages, features, revisions and timeline. Quotes are valid for 30 days. Work that isn't in the quote is only added after you approve a separate price.

Free previews and consultations

Consultations and website previews are free, with no obligation to buy.

Payment schedule

  • 50% deposit to book your project and start the design.
  • 50% balance once you've approved the finished website, before it goes live on your domain.

For larger projects we can agree payments in stages (milestones), written into your quote. Monthly services, such as maintenance or marketing, are paid at the start of each month.

How to pay

We accept bank transfer to accounts in Nigeria, the UK and the USA, and payment through Payoneer, Cleva and Wise. The payment details are on your invoice. Nigerian clients are invoiced in naira (₦); other clients in pounds (£) or US dollars ($). Please confirm payment details with us directly before sending money, and never pay into an account that isn't on your official invoice.

Invoices

Invoices are due within 7 days. If a payment is late, we pause work until it arrives, and the delivery date moves by the same amount of time.

Third-party costs

Your domain name, hosting, premium plugins, stock photos and other third-party services are listed separately in your quote. They're registered in your name wherever possible, so you own them. Most renew every year, and we remind you before renewal.

Ownership

Once the final payment is made, the website, its design and its files belong to you.
