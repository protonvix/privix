Privacy

When you send a project brief, we receive your name, business details, email address, WhatsApp number and the answers you choose. We use them only to reply to you and to prepare your quote.

Your brief is delivered to our email inbox through FormSubmit, an email delivery service, and to our WhatsApp if you choose to send it there. We don't sell or share your details, and we don't add you to mailing lists.

To see, change or delete your details, email vixautomations@gmail.com and we'll do it within 7 days.
