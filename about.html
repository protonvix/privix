About Vix Studio

We build websites that make businesses look as good as they are

Vix Studio is the web design studio of Vix Automations, founded by Promise Felix. We work with businesses in Nigeria and the UK, especially car dealers, car rental companies, real estate agents, restaurants and service businesses.

Why we started

Many good businesses lose customers online without knowing it. Their website is outdated, slow on phones, broken, or missing altogether, so buyers who search for them find nothing they can trust. We started Vix Studio to fix that: modern, fast websites that win trust at first glance and turn visitors into enquiries.

What we do

  • New websites designed from scratch for your business and your customers.
  • Redesigns of outdated, slow or broken websites, including recovering sites whose domain has expired.
  • Interactive features like 3D showcases, car and property listings, booking forms and WhatsApp enquiries.
  • Business automation with GoHighLevel: lead capture, follow-ups, reminders and pipelines, through Vix Automations.
  • TikTok and social media marketing to bring more people to your new website.

How we work

  • Fixed quote first. You know the full price before any work starts.
  • You approve the design. We don't build until you're happy with how it looks.
  • You own everything. Your domain, website files and content belong to you, with no lock-in.
  • Clear communication. One point of contact, on WhatsApp or email, from first message to launch.

Where we are

We work remotely with clients from Lagos, Nigeria and London, United Kingdom. Meetings happen on WhatsApp, phone or video call at a time that suits you.
